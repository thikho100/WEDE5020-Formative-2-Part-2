Our Dental Services

Dental Check-ups

Examinations of the teeth and gums to identify problems early.

Expected time: 30 minutes

Teeth Cleaning

Removal of plaque, tartar and surface stains from the teeth.

Expected time: 30 - 45 minutes

Preventive Care

Oral hygiene advice and treatment that helps prevent tooth decay.

Expected time: 30 minutes

Dental Fillings

Treatment for cavities that restores damaged teeth.

Expected time: 45 - 60 minutes

Tooth Extractions

Removal of teeth that cannot be repaired or are causing pain.

Expected time: 45 - 60 minutes

Emergency Dental Care

Urgent attention for toothache, broken teeth and dental injuries.

Expected time: 30 - 45 minutes
