Frequently Asked Questions

Which payment methods do you accept?

Patients can pay by card or medical aid.

How do I cancel or change an appointment?

Call the dentistry on 081 86042 35. Please contact us as early as possible so the time can be given to another patient.

What are the operating hours?

Monday to Friday: 08:00 - 17:00

Saturday: 08:00 - 13:00

Sunday and public holidays: Closed

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring your identification, medical aid details if you have medical aid and any information the dentist asked for.

Is the appointment confirmed immediately?

The booking is first sent to the dentistry. The receptionist will confirm it or contact you if the time needs to be changed.
